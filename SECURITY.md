# Security policy

Please read the site’s [security disclosure policy](/security/) before reporting a vulnerability. The reporting contact is published in [security.txt](/.well-known/security.txt) after the maintainer replaces the explicit pre-production placeholder.

Good-faith reports are welcome. Do not perform destructive testing, denial-of-service testing, social engineering, or access other people’s data. This policy does not authorize testing against Vercel, GitHub, ISPs, or other third-party infrastructure.
